Grace Kaimburi

I use machine learning to find and test climate solutions, from materials that capture combustion pollutants to models that flag health risk early where data is scarce.

I am a master's student in Engineering Artificial Intelligence at Carnegie Mellon University Africa in Kigali. I also work on the policy side, including marine policy and port health at AGNES, because a model that nobody can act on does not cut emissions or protect a patient. I am preparing applications for doctoral study in AI for the environment.
