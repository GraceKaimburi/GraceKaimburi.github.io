About

I am a master's student in Engineering Artificial Intelligence at Carnegie Mellon University Africa in Kigali. My research uses machine learning to find and test climate solutions, from materials that capture combustion pollutants to models that flag health risk early where data is scarce.

I also work on the policy side, including marine policy and port health at AGNES, because a model that nobody can act on does not cut emissions or protect a patient. I am preparing applications for doctoral study in AI for the environment.

Climate solutions

Machine learning surrogates for density functional theory, applied to adsorbents for combustion pollutants.

Core machine learning

Transfer learning for low resource settings, benchmarking, and surrogate modelling.

Climate policy and health

Marine and port health policy, climate risk and health prediction, and youth climate policy.
